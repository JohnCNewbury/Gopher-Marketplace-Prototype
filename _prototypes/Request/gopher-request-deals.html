How Deals Works

Deals are delivered by neighbors, not algorithms. Please keep in mind there is no tip screen — your Offer to a Gopher is the pay, and 100% of it goes to the worker who chooses your request. Nothing is auto-assigned: fair offers get picked up fast, low offers tend to sit unclaimed. Deals was built so everyone in the deal wins — the merchant, you, and the worker doing the run.
