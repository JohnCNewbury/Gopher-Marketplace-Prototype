Why errand rides are better here

Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that makes you re-book between every stop. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

🤝

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — one quick two-stop hop or a whole afternoon of errands.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. One fare agreed up front, with no per-leg surge for the loop.

🔁

Booked to done

The driver you pick takes you stop to stop, waits while you run each errand, helps with the bags, and brings you home — the same familiar face for your usual rounds.
