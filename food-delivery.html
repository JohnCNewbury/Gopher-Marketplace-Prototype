Why food delivery is better here

Three things the apps won’t do

Gopher isn’t another delivery app taking a cut in the middle. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

The food costs what it costs in-store — no app markup. For the delivery, you set the offer, and your Gopher keeps every cent of it. It’s a fair wage you decide, not a mystery fee stack.

🏪

Order direct & local

The restaurant is always paid directly and keeps its full margin. You’re ordering from the real spot down the street — which is exactly why your favorites can afford to stay open.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A Gopher you pick by name and rating owns the whole trip — counter to your door. You can message, call, and track them live, and they’re accountable in-app the entire way.
