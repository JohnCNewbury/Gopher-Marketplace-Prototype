Why a Gopher for returns

Off your plate, and proven

Gopher isn’t a courier dispatch or a membership. It’s a neighbor clearing that pile for you — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Set the offer for the run and your Gopher keeps every cent. Prepaid return labels cost you nothing; any postage you do owe is paid at cost, straight to the carrier — Gopher never marks it up.

📸

Proof, not guesswork

When every box is dropped, your Gopher sends a photo of the drop-off receipt. No more wondering whether the return made it — your refund clock starts the same day.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A Gopher you pick by name and rating grabs the pile from your door, prints the label, and owns the whole trip — counter, line, and all. Track them the entire way.
