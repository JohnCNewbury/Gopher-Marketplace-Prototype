Why a Gopher for store runs

Any store, off your list

Gopher isn’t a retail app with a curated catalog. It’s a neighbor running to the store for you — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Your items cost the real shelf price, paid straight to the store — no per-item markup. For the run, you set the offer, and your Gopher keeps every cent of it.

🏬

Any store, paid direct

Send a Gopher to any store — or to grab a curbside or in-store pickup order. The store gets its real price, directly; Gopher takes no cut of your stuff.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A Gopher you pick by name and rating grabs it, texts you a photo when there’s a choice, and brings it to your door. Track them the whole way.
