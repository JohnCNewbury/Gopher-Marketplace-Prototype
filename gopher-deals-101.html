Getting started

Get listed — it's free

Registration is one form with three parts: your business, your first deal, and the owner details that become your Gopher login. Hit I'm a merchant on the Deals page and you'll see this:

The registration form — business info first, your deal second, your owner details last. A few minutes, start to finish.

  1. Business info. Name, logo (square transparent PNG works best — it displays on a white background, so avoid all-white or very light artwork), tagline, address, and your category. Food truck? Tick business address is mobile. Your logo shows in the live preview straight away; we’ll collect the file itself when we set up your listing.
  2. Online ordering. Drop in the website where customers order. No online ordering? Check the box and we'll follow up so customers still know how to order from you.
  3. Your deal. The headline customers tap on — keep it short, and make it trackable (a code like GOPHER makes redemptions easy to count).
  4. Business owner verification. This is the standard Personal Info for every Gopher account — it sets up your owner login, so you use the same details to sign in afterwards. Name, date of birth, mobile, email and address. You’ll add a profile photo the first time you sign in.

Verifying it’s you — start with your mobile

Registering creates a real Gopher account, so we confirm how to reach you. Your mobile is the only step everyone does — what happens after it depends on whether you already have an account, and we work that out for you.

  1. Your mobile. Tap Verify beside the phone field and we’ll text you a 6-digit code. Type it in, tap Confirm, and the field switches to ✓ Phone verified.
  2. Your email — only if you’re new to Gopher. A Verify button sits beside the email field, exactly like the phone one. Tap it, we’ll email a 6-digit code, enter it and tap Confirm for ✓ Email verified. That button turns into Resend if the code doesn’t arrive.

Already have a Gopher account? The email step disappears — you’ll see ✓ Email verified straight away, because your address was confirmed when you signed up. One code, and you’re done.

⚡
Already use Gopher? We’ll spot it — you don’t have to tell usJust verify your mobile. If that number is already on a Gopher account, we recognise it and fill in your name, date of birth and email for you — no second account, and nothing to remember. The I’m already a Gopher user tick-box at the top is a shortcut, not a requirement: forget it and you’ll be recognised anyway.

Getting started

Sign in to your portal

Deals is passwordless — nothing to remember. Choose Merchant or Service Provider, then sign in with a one-time code.

  1. Open the Deals sign-in and pick Email or SMS.
  2. Enter the email or mobile number on your account and tap Send code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code and tap Sign in. Tick Remember me to skip this next time.

Your Deals portal

Submit a new deal

One green button — + Submit new deal — and one form. We pre-fill everything already on file, and the phone on the right shows your card exactly as customers will see it, updating live as you type.

The live preview is the real deals feed — your offer, logo, name and tagline update as you type.

  • Pick the business & location — we pre-fill from your last deal there; edit anything that's changed.
  • Write the offer — this is the headline customers tap on. Short and trackable wins.
  • Submit — every deal is reviewed before it goes live, and you'll see it under My Deals with its status (Live or In review).

Getting featured

Feature my business the spotlight

Every month, the top spot on each Deals category card goes up for bid — and the standings are completely open. You always see the current top bid; nothing is blind.

Live standings — the badge marks the projected Featured Deal, and the sticker tells you when your bid is leading your category.

  • Bidding closes on the 20th of each month; winners go live on the 1st of the next month.
  • You bid on your own category card — a restaurant can't bid on Retail Merchants. Yours is marked; the others show standings only.
  • Top bid per category is featured across all Gopher Marketplace apps and web platforms for the month.
  • Top bid overall headlines as the Featured Deal — that's the "Projected featured deal" badge moving around the board.
  • Bidding is free; only winning costs — placing a bid commits you to pay if it holds, and you're charged only on the closing day. Outbid? You pay nothing.

Good to know

Service providers, you're in too

Local pros — power washers, landscapers, handymen, and every service business in between — offer deals too, but the path is different from a merchant's in three ways:

  • Eligibility is automatic, and there's a bar. You're in once you're a verified tier (Elite, Elite+, or Pro) with 20+ completed service jobs and a 4.75★ average over your last 20 service jobs. Delivery, Ride Sharing and Other jobs don't count toward either. Nothing to buy, no application — it switches on the moment you qualify.
  • You offer your deal in the Gopher Go app, not here. The form on the Deals page is a quick eligibility check — we look you up by your Gopher ID and come back with terms. The deal itself you submit from your Go dashboard, where your details are already on file.
  • Your deals appear under Local Service Provider Deals, and your category card for featured bidding is Service Providers.
