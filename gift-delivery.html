Why a Gopher for gifts

Thoughtful, local, same-day

Gopher isn’t a catalog of mass-produced baskets. It’s a neighbor grabbing a real gift from a local shop — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Your gift costs the shop’s real price, paid directly — no shipping, handling, or “gifting” fees. For the run, you set the offer, and your Gopher keeps every cent.

🎁

From any local shop

The boutique, the bookshop, the bakery down the street — pick the gift yourself or let your Gopher choose something thoughtful in your budget. The shop keeps 100%.

✍️

Delivered with a note

A Gopher you pick by name and rating adds your handwritten note, can hand it to the person for the surprise, and sends a photo the moment it arrives.
