Certifications

Forklift, and everything like it

Equipment certification is the one thing on a warehouse post you should never assume, so here is the honest version.

Ask for it on the post. Verify it on arrival.

Plenty of Gophers run powered equipment and say so on their profile. Gopher does not issue that certification — it comes from a training provider or an employer, and running powered equipment on your floor is regulated. Put the requirement on the post, confirm the card before you accept, and apply whatever your site already asks of a visiting operator.

  • Equipment — Sit-down, stand-up reach, order picker, walkie rider, pallet jack. They are not interchangeable, and an operator knows it.
  • Verification — Whether you will check the certification on arrival, and exactly what you want to see.
  • PPE — Steel toes, hi-vis, hard hat — and whether the worker brings it or you supply it.
Read this before you post equipment work. Every Elite/Elite+ worker on the platform has passed a background check, and profiles show ratings and past jobs — none of which is an equipment certification, and Connect does not verify one on your behalf. Site safety, training records, and supervision on your floor stay yours. If your operation has a hard requirement here, make sure your request states that clearly. You can always confirm with documentation prior to accepting a worker as well.
