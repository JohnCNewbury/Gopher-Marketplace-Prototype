Your privacy

Biometric information & your consent

TrustShield is voluntary. You never need it to create an account or use Gopher. If you choose it, we collect a photo of the front and back of your government-issued ID and a selfie — the selfie may be “biometric information” under laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA).

We use it for one thing: confirming the ID is yours and that you meet the legal age for age-restricted orders, and reducing fraud. We never sell it or profit from it.

We destroy it when that purpose is met, or within three (3) years of your last interaction with Gopher, whichever comes first — and within thirty (30) days if you withdraw from TrustShield or delete your account, unless the law requires us to keep it.

You give consent when you accept our Terms of Service at signup and when you submit an age-restricted request. You can withdraw it anytime at support@gophergo.io. Full details are in our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Good to know

TrustShield questions, answered.

Is TrustShield really free?
Yes. Gopher TrustShield is our free identity verification, powered by IDenfy, and it’s open to everyone — there’s no cost to add it to your profile.
Why do I need TrustShield ID verification?
ID setup is required for age-restricted deliveries, and TrustShield keeps the platform compliant while reassuring both customers and workers that it isn’t being misused. Once your TrustShield is authenticated you don’t need to enter it again, ordering gets much faster, and you save $1 on every age-restricted request thereafter.
Do I still have to show my ID at delivery?
Yes. For age-restricted items you’ll always show a valid physical ID in person so it can be matched. If you’re TrustShield verified, your worker will already see your ID and photo in their app — the in-person check just confirms the match.
How long does approval take?
After you submit your info, you’ll be notified of your approval within minutes.
Why can’t I change my name for TrustShield?
If you set up your Gopher Request account with an alias, you won’t be able to apply for TrustShield, since the name has to match your ID. To keep your previous requests, contact us at support@gophergo.io and we’ll help you update the name on your account. Please don’t email a photo of your ID. If your account has no activity, you can delete it and sign up again — just double-check your information before saving your profile.
