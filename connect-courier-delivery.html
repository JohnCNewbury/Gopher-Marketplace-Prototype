Vehicles & proof

What a courier actually brings

Delivery is where an assumption gets expensive, so here is the honest version.

Say the vehicle. Say the proof.

Gopher couriers drive their own vehicles, and what they drive varies — sedan to cargo van. Nothing is dispatched with a guaranteed vehicle class, so put the requirement on the post and confirm it before you accept.

  • Vehicle — Say whether it needs a van, a truck bed, or just a trunk. Cargo dimensions belong in the scope.
  • Handling — Temperature, fragility, upright-only, hand truck. A courier sizes the run before accepting it.
  • Proof — A photo at drop-off is standard. If you need a signature or a named recipient, say so on the post.
Read this if the delivery is regulated. Age-restricted items — alcohol and the like — run through Gopher's age-restricted flow, where the courier checks ID at drop-off. Controlled substances, hazardous materials, and anything that needs a specific carrier endorsement are not general-courier work — ask us before you post one rather than after. Every Elite/Elite+ worker on the platform has passed a background check; that is a separate thing from a regulated-goods credential.
