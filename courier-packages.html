Why a Gopher for packages

Dropped off, and proven

Gopher isn’t a courier dispatch or a membership. It’s a neighbor running it for you — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Set the offer for the run and your Gopher keeps every cent. Any postage or shipping is paid at cost, straight to the carrier — Gopher never marks it up.

📸

Proof, not guesswork

When the drop-off is done, your Gopher sends a photo of the receipt or the tracking number. No more wondering whether your return actually made it.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A Gopher you pick by name and rating grabs it from your door and owns the whole trip — counter, line, and all. Track them the entire way.
