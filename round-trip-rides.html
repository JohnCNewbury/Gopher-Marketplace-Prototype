Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that drops you and disappears. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

🤝

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a quick wait-and-return errand or a driver who stays the whole appointment.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. One fare agreed up front, with no second surge for the trip back.

🔁

Booked to done

The driver you pick takes you there, waits as long as the stop takes, and brings you home — one familiar face, never a scramble for a ride back.
