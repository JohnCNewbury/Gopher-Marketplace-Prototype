Why a Gopher for flowers

Local, fresh, hand-delivered

Gopher isn’t a wire service marking up a far-off bouquet. It’s a neighbor carrying fresh flowers from a local shop — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Your flowers cost the florist’s real price, paid directly — no service fees or wire-service markup. For the run, you set the offer, and your Gopher keeps every cent.

🌸

The florist keeps it all

You order direct from a local shop, so they keep 100% of the price — no national service skimming a commission off a neighborhood florist.

💐

Delivered with care

A Gopher you pick by name and rating carries them fresh — not left to wilt on a porch — can hand them to the person with your note, and sends a photo the moment they arrive.
