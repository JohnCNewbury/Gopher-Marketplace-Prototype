Access & supplies

The two things that derail a clean

Almost every cleaning job that goes wrong goes wrong for one of two reasons, and both are fixable on the post.

Say who brings what, and how they get in.

A cleaner sizes your job from the post. If supplies and access are not in it they are guessing — and a guess is what turns a two-hour clean into a phone call from your parking lot at 9 PM.

  • Supplies — On site, or the cleaner brings their own? Vacuum, mop, chemicals, liners, and paper all count.
  • Access — Keys, lockbox, badge, alarm code, gate, loading dock — and who to call when one of them does not work.
  • Windows — The hours the space is genuinely available, including how late is too late for the neighbours.
On “vetted” — here is exactly what that means. Every Elite/Elite+ worker on the platform has passed a background check, and you can see ratings and past jobs before you accept anyone. That is not the same as a bonded janitorial firm carrying its own commercial policy. If your building requires a certificate of insurance, cleaning-specific credentials, or a named vendor on file, use Choose which workers are eligible and select Only send to Gopher Elite and Pros — when workers accept your job, you’ll see their Gopher Pro badge clearly.
