Day-of reality

The event does not move, so staff it like it

Events are the one category where being a day early costs nothing and being an hour late costs everything.

Post early, name the call time, and leave yourself a seat.

Everything on Connect is opt-in — a worker accepts your post, they are not assigned to it. That is what keeps the quality up, and it also means the fill is yours to manage, especially for a date that cannot slip. You also choose how the crew comes together — a Connect-only call.

  • Crew or individual — your choice — Under Labor management, either select each worker individually and approve every face at your venue, or hire a contractor to assemble your crew and get one point of contact for the whole call. Same post, either way.
  • Call time — Put the actual call time on the post, not the door time. Workers plan their day around the number you give them.
  • Buffer — For a hard date, post more seats than the minimum and post them further out than feels necessary.
  • Repeat crews — Save the people who showed up early to MY Gophers and prioritize them for the next date.
We will not promise you a crew we cannot see. How quickly an event shift fills depends on how many Gophers are active near your venue that day, and that varies by market — Gopher iQ will show you what your area actually looks like before you commit to a client. If the event is large, load-bearing, or on a holiday weekend, talk to us before you post it rather than the morning of. And post as far ahead as you can: Connect can staff on demand, but time is what makes on-demand come out right — the earlier a shift is up, the more of the local workforce ever sees it. Being able to post late is not a reason to.
How it works

Posted to doors open

1

Describe the call

Call time, headcount, venue, load-in access, dress code, and what the shift involves. A photo of the room helps.

2

Set what you'll pay

Your offer is what each worker earns — 100% of it. Or open it to bids and compare.

3

Pick who takes it

Review profiles, ratings, and past jobs. Hire, consider, or deny — and save the good ones to MY Gophers.

4

Confirm when it's done

Confirm and rate on completion, which releases payment. Something not right? Dispute instead and give them a chance to fix it.
