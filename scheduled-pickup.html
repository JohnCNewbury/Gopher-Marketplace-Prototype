Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app whose ‘scheduled’ ride is really just a reminder to gamble later. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

🤝

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a one-off booking for next week or a standing pickup every weekday.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. The fare is agreed up front, with no surge at pickup time.

The driver you pick commits to your exact time — one-off or recurring — and shows up reliably, the same familiar face every trip.
