Why appointment rides are better here

Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that surges or cancels right when you can’t be late. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

🤝

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a single can’t-miss appointment or a standing weekly booking.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. The fare is agreed up front, with no surge when you’re in a hurry.

⏰

Booked to done

The driver you pick is there on time, waits or returns if you need, and can be the same reliable face you rebook for every appointment.
