Why a Gopher for pickup

Order direct, eat hot

Gopher isn’t a delivery app taking a cut of your favorite restaurant. It’s a neighbor grabbing your order — on three simple rules.

💵

You pay what you want

Your food costs the real menu price, paid straight to the restaurant — no app markup. For the run, you set the offer, and your Gopher keeps every cent of it.

🏪

The kitchen keeps it all

You order direct — by phone, site, or in person — so the restaurant keeps 100% of the menu price. No app skimming a commission off your favorite spot.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A Gopher you pick by name and rating grabs your ready order and brings it straight over — no stacked orders, no detours, no cold fries. Track them the whole way.
