Medical Appointment Rides

A reliable ride to your appointment,

— and home after.

The check-up across town, the physical therapy you go to weekly, the procedure where they won’t let you drive yourself home — getting there and back shouldn’t be the hard part. A vetted local driver, booked in advance, gets you to your appointment on time, waits if you ask, and takes you home after, for a fare agreed up front. This is a regular, non-emergency ride — not medical transport or an ambulance; for an emergency call 911, and if you need medical care or supervision during the trip, use a licensed medical transport service. You pick a patient, familiar driver by name, rating, and reviews.

Why these rides are better here

Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that surges or cancels on a day you can’t miss. It’s a local marketplace for regular, non-emergency rides, built on three simple rules.

🤝

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a one-off appointment or a standing weekly ride to treatment.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. The fare is agreed up front, with no surge on a stressful day.

⏰

Booked to done

The driver you pick gets you there on time, waits if you ask, and takes you home after — a patient, familiar face for every visit. A regular ride to and from appointments — not medical transport; for emergencies call 911.
