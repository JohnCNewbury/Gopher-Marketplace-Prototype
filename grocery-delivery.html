Why a Gopher for groceries

Real prices, real shopping

Gopher isn’t a grocery app marking up your cart in the middle. It’s a neighbor shopping for you, on three simple rules.

🧾

You pay what you want

Groceries cost the real shelf price — the actual receipt, paid to the store, no per-item markup. For the shopping run, you set the offer and your Gopher keeps every cent of it.

🥬

Shopped with care

Your Gopher picks your produce like it’s for their own kitchen and texts you before swapping anything that’s out of stock. No mystery subs, no bruised bananas, no surprises at the door.

🚗

Pickup to perfect

A shopper you pick by name and rating owns the whole trip — aisle to your door. Track them live, chat along the way, and save your favorite for next time.
