Why school rides are better here

Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that wasn’t built for kids and puts a different stranger at the wheel. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules — with you in control.

🛡️

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a one-off pickup when you’re stuck or a standing ride every school day.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. The fare is agreed up front, with no surge on the school-run schedule.

📍

Booked to done

A driver you approve — vetted and background-checked — picks your child up from a verified spot, you follow the whole trip live, and you get an alert the moment they’re dropped safely with you or an approved adult.
