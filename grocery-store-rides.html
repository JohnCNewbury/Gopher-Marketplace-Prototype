Grocery Store Rides

To the store, and home with the bags,

— all in one trip.

No car, a big weekly haul, or just too many bags for the bus — the grocery run shouldn’t mean two rides and a wrestling match with the cart. A vetted local driver takes you to the store, waits while you shop, and brings you and every bag home — lending a hand to load up and carry them in — for one fare agreed up front. No two surge-priced trips, no full cart and no car back, no cut taken from your driver. You pick your driver by name, rating, and reviews, and keep the one who makes the weekly run easy.

Why grocery rides are better here

Three things a rideshare app won’t do

Gopher isn’t a rideshare app that drops you and leaves you with a full cart and no car. It’s a local marketplace built on three simple rules.

🤝

Hassle free & haggle free

Make an offer up front or ask for instant bids. Either way you’ll experience the easiest way to find a great driver ever — a quick corner-store run or a big monthly stock-up.

💵

Paid directly

Your driver is paid directly and keeps 100% of the fare — 0% commission. One fare agreed up front, with no second surge for the trip home.

🛒

Booked to done

The driver you pick takes you to the store, waits while you shop, and helps load the bags and carry them to your door — the same familiar face for every weekly run.
