Qualifications

How we talk about credentials

Trade work is where vague claims cause real problems, so here is the honest version.

Pro-verified: licensed, bonded, or insured

The Pro credential confirms a worker holds the credentials relevant to their trade. Which ones a given Pro carries is shown on their profile — so you can require what your job genuinely needs instead of assuming.

  • Licensed — holds the state or local license their trade requires.
  • Bonded — carries a surety bond covering the work performed.
  • Insured — carries liability coverage for work on your premises.
Read this carefully if the work is license-gated. Trades such as electrical and plumbing are licensed at the state level, and not every qualified handyman holds one — nor does every job require it. Gopher does not claim that every worker is licensed. For license-gated work, set that requirement on the post and confirm the credential on the worker's profile before you accept. Every Elite/Elite+ worker on the platform has passed a background check; that is a separate check from a trade license. Gopher Pros will have a trade license, which you confirm before accepting that Pro.
